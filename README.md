# Thuthuka Nathi

**Your skills, verified.**

A mobile-first web app that helps young South Africans turn informal experience into a verified skills profile and connect it to real work opportunities.

Built for the **FNB App of the Year Hackathon 2026**, under the track **Youth employment and work readiness**.

🔗 **Live demo:** https://thuthuka-nathi.vercel.app

## The problem

Many young people have real skills they can't prove: tutoring neighbours' children, helping at a family stall, or learning Excel on YouTube. With no CV, no formal references and little mobile data, they are shut out of opportunities they could do well.

## Our solution

1. **Skills capture:** the user describes what they have done in their own words, and the app turns it into a structured skills list.
2. **Verification:** the user asks someone who knows them, such as a relative or a client, to confirm a skill with one tap. Confirmed skills earn a **Verified** badge.
3. **Opportunity matching:** the app matches the user to entry-level jobs, learnerships and micro-internships, and shows the real cost of applying (taxi fare and data).
4. **Skills-gap insight:** the app shows how learning one more skill would unlock more opportunities.
